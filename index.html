Common Questions

DFW Roofing FAQ

Hail damage is almost always invisible from the ground. You won’t see cracked or missing shingles. What you’ll see is granule loss, soft spots in the decking, and damaged ridge caps. The only reliable way to know is to have a HAAG-certified inspector on the roof. That’s what the free inspection is for.

Most Texas homeowner policies cover hail and wind damage to the roof. The key is making sure the damage is thoroughly documented before any decisions are made. We provide a complete written inspection report so you and your insurer have an accurate picture of what your roof needs. For questions about coverage, speak with your insurance agent or a licensed public adjuster.

Most residential roof replacements in DFW are completed in a single day. T-Rock’s experienced crews handle the tear-off and installation in one visit. The timeline from first inspection to finished roof typically runs 2–6 weeks depending on materials, scheduling, and your specific situation.
