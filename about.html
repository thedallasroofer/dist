What You're Not Going to Get From Me

There are a lot of roofers in DFW. Here's what separates a call to me from calling anyone else.

No Storm Chasing

I'm not in your neighborhood because a hail event just happened and I'm working a leads list. T-Rock has been in DFW for 65 years. This is where we work, not just where we show up after storms.

No Deductible Waivers

Any contractor offering to waive or "cover" your deductible is breaking Texas law (HB 2102). I won't offer it, and you shouldn't want it. It's a sign corners are being cut elsewhere.

No Insurance Negotiation

Under Texas law, I can't negotiate your claim. And I won't try. What I can do is make sure your damage is fully documented before the adjuster shows up. That's the honest version of helping.

No Runaround

You call me, I answer. You have a question mid-job, you text me. You want to know what's happening with scheduling, I'll tell you. That's how this works.

No Guesswork on Your Roof

We don't patch and hope. A proper inspection finds the real problem, not just the visible symptom. The repair is done right, with the right materials, by a licensed crew.

No Pressure

If your roof doesn't need work, I'll tell you that. If it needs repair when another contractor might push replacement, I'll tell you that too. You deserve an honest answer.
