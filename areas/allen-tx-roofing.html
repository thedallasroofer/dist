What Allen Homeowners Need to Know About Storm Season

Collin County Is in the Core of the Hail Corridor

Allen, Plano, McKinney, and Frisco all sit in the northern section of DFW's hail corridor. One of the most active hail zones in the United States. Storms that push northeast through the Metroplex regularly bring significant hail to Allen neighborhoods from March through June, and the city's flat terrain gives hail nowhere to lose energy before it hits your roof.

Two Generations of Roofs, Two Different Risk Profiles

Allen's older neighborhoods. Shadow Lakes, Glendover Park, Rivercrest Estates. Are carrying roofs installed in the late 1990s and early 2000s. Those roofs are near or past their expected lifespan. A moderate hail event that a newer roof absorbs without visible damage can compromise an aging 3-tab roof significantly. North Allen's newer homes aren't immune either. Impact damage on architectural shingles often requires a trained eye to catch.

Hail Damage Hides Below the Surface

Shingle bruising, granule loss, and underlayment cracks rarely show from the street. Allen homeowners regularly discover roof damage only when a stain appears on an interior ceiling. Often weeks after the storm. By that point, water has already worked into the decking. Don't wait for visible interior signs; get a storm damage inspection done right after the event.

Storm Damage Is Typically a Covered Peril

Hail and wind damage from a storm event is typically covered under standard homeowner's insurance. Though coverage depends on your specific policy, deductible, and the documented cause of damage. The best thing you can do before calling your insurer is get a thorough inspection completed so you have clear, documented information to share with your carrier.

Know Who You're Calling After a Storm

Storm chasers target Allen neighborhoods after major hail events. Texas has no statewide roofing license requirement, so anyone can knock on your door. Always ask for proof of insurance, check their BBB rating before signing anything, and never sign an Assignment of Benefits. It transfers your insurance rights to the contractor. T-Rock has been in DFW for 65+ years; we're not going anywhere after the storm passes.

Act Quickly: Before Additional Rain Events

Getting a documented inspection done fast. Before more rain hits a compromised roof. Means the damage record is clear and tied to the storm that caused it. Waiting lets water infiltration progress into the decking and insulation, turning what should be a clean roof replacement into something more complex. Call the moment you suspect damage.
