Why Prosper Homeowners Can't Ignore Hail Season

North Collin County Is Prime Hail Territory

Prosper sits in the upper portion of Collin County. In the path of severe weather systems that push through DFW from spring through early summer. The northern Metroplex sees frequent hail events, and Prosper is directly in the corridor.

New Roofs Still Get Damaged

Most homes in Prosper were built in the last 10–15 years, which means many homeowners assume their roof is "fine." But new shingles bruise under significant hail impact just like any other roofing material. And the damage is just as invisible from the ground.

HOA Requirements Add Complexity

Communities like Windsong Ranch and Light Farms have strict architectural review requirements for roofing materials, colors, and often specific installation standards. Getting HOA approval wrong means starting over. We build your scope to HOA spec from day one.

Storm Damage Is Typically a Covered Peril

Hail and wind damage from a storm event is typically covered under standard homeowner's insurance. Though coverage depends on your specific policy, deductible, and the documented cause of damage. For questions about your coverage, speak with your insurance agent or a licensed public adjuster.

Storm Chasers Follow the Growth

Fast-growing areas like Prosper attract out-of-town contractors after every major storm. Texas has no statewide roofing license requirement. So almost anyone can show up at your door. Ask for proof of liability insurance and workers' comp, verify their BBB rating, and never sign an Assignment of Benefits. T-Rock has been in DFW for 65+ years.

Your Builder Warranty May Not Cover Hail

Many Prosper homeowners assume their builder warranty covers storm damage. It typically doesn't. Builder warranties cover workmanship defects, not weather events. Storm damage is a homeowner's insurance matter, not a builder warranty claim.
