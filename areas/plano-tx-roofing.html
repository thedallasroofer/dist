Why Plano Homeowners Need to Stay on Top of Roof Condition

Established Neighborhoods, Aging Roofs

Many Plano homes were built in the 1980s and 1990s, with roofs that have seen 20–30+ years of DFW storm seasons. An aging roof that looks fine from the street can have underlayment and decking that's been quietly deteriorating through repeated hail and heat cycles.

Plano Is No Stranger to North Texas Hail Storms

Plano and Collin County regularly experience severe spring and early-summer thunderstorms, including hail and high winds. When a storm moves through quickly, even established neighborhoods can see roof damage that is not obvious from the ground.

Damage Is Often Invisible from the Ground

Granule loss, bruised underlayment, and cracked shingles don't announce themselves from the driveway. Many Plano homeowners only discover roof damage when a water stain shows up on an interior ceiling. Weeks or months after the storm that caused it.

Storm Damage May Be Covered by Insurance

Hail and wind damage may be covered under many homeowner's insurance policies, depending on your policy terms, deductible, exclusions, and the carrier's inspection findings. A properly documented inspection gives you clear roof-condition information to share with your insurance carrier and can help you understand what the T-Rock team observed before the carrier's inspection.

Watch for Storm Chasers After Major Events

Out-of-town contractors descend on Plano after every significant hail event. Always verify the company's insurance, local track record, written scope, warranty terms, and any roofing credentials or association memberships before signing anything. Work with a contractor who's been in DFW long-term.
